# EPL / La Liga Divergence Lab: image guide

The 14 images for the site redesign (the "Matchday Programme" look).
Work top to bottom and tick the boxes as you go.

**The look in one line:** a printed matchday programme. Two-ink risograph
illustrations (pitch green and La Liga red) on bright paper, bold wide type,
and the arms shown as programme "player cards". It's deliberately the
opposite of UCL's floodlit night: daytime, printed and tactile instead of
cinematic.

---

## Step 0: Set up (5 minutes)

- [ ] Make a folder on your desktop called **`epl_images`**. Save every image there.
- [ ] Pick a tool:
  - **ChatGPT** for the **avatars** (Part B). It holds one style across a set best.
  - **Gemini or ChatGPT** for the **scenes** (Part A).
- [ ] Every prompt below is complete. Copy the whole grey block each time and don't shorten it.

### Reject and regenerate if any of these appear
- ❌ Any text, letters or numbers, including on shirts, scoreboards, tickets or programmes. AI garbles them, and I add text in code.
- ❌ The Premier League lion, the La Liga logo, or anything that looks like either.
- ❌ Real club crests, badges, kit sponsors or a recognisable kit (e.g. a red shirt with white sleeves).
- ❌ A real, recognisable stadium (Anfield, Bernabéu, Camp Nou, etc.) or a real player's face.
- ❌ Colours beyond the two inks and their overlap. A third colour, gradients or a "glossy 3D" look means the style has slipped.
- ✅ If only a small part is wrong, reply in the same chat: *"Same image, but remove the [thing]. Keep everything else identical."*

### Saving
- Download each one at the **largest size**, as **PNG**.
- Rename it to the **exact filename** in its step and put it in `epl_images`.

### 👉 Open a NEW chat (ChatGPT recommended). Name it "EPL avatars".
Make all 6 in this **one chat**. Starting a new chat partway through resets the style.

How it works:
1. Make **B1** first and regenerate until you love it. It's the template for the set.
2. From **B2** on, **attach `avatar-a.png`** to each message as the style reference.
3. Each one appears on the site as a programme "player card". I add the frame, name and numbers in code, so the portrait itself stays plain.

If a background comes out patterned, coloured or white, reply: *"Same portrait, plain off-white paper background #F4F6F3, nothing else behind him/her."*

The shared style line (already in each prompt):

> Two-colour risograph portrait using only emerald green (#00895A) and bright red (#E2423B) inks, overprinting to deep brown, on plain off-white paper (#F4F6F3). Head and shoulders, three-quarter view, centred, same framing every time, coarse halftone shading, slight ink misregistration, bold flat shapes, 1960s matchday-programme player-portrait style.
